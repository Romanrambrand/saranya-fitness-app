This is a general wellness tracker, not medical advice. Begin only when your healthcare professional has cleared you for exercise. Postpartum recovery varies; progress by comfort, not by the calendar. Stop and seek guidance if you notice pain, pelvic heaviness/pressure, urine leakage, bleeding, or abdominal bulging/doming. If you had a C-section, complications, pelvic-floor symptoms, or diastasis recti, ask your clinician or pelvic-health physiotherapist to adapt your plan.

Hydration · தண்ணீர்

Keep water nearby and sip regularly, guided by thirst and your clinician's advice. Needs can be higher while breastfeeding or in hot weather; do not force a fixed amount.

தண்ணீரை அருகில் வைத்துக் கொண்டு தாகத்திற்கேற்ப குடியுங்கள். தாய்ப்பால் கொடுக்கும் போது தேவைகள் அதிகரிக்கலாம்; கட்டாய அளவு குடிக்க வேண்டாம்.

Nourishment · சத்தான உணவு

Choose regular, satisfying meals with a protein source, vegetables or fruit, and fiber-rich grains/beans. Avoid skipping meals, detoxes, or severe restriction—especially while breastfeeding.
